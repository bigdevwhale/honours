## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-26

### Added
- The commission: a daily agenda of small deeds to tick (repeats, deeds that fit the hour and season, new ones), the full list of 337 deeds in seven ministries with search, and badges of your own.
- The ceremony: brass fanfare, the badge rising on velvet with a gloss that follows the phone's tilt, the order typed out, the stamp.
- Badge classes: bronze, silver at 5, gold at 15, the Honoured title at 30.
- Felt pennants of 16 badges on a wallpapered wall; badges can be moved by holding them.
- The Order of the Day Survived and 13 secret badges that find you.
- The workshop: shape, enamels, a stamped pictogram or a 9×9 drawing, and your own decree.
- Award sheets for other people, shared as a picture with a stamp and a signature.
- The record: entries by day, the collection, the yearly report.
- One evening notification, only on a day without awards; EN and RU; demo build.
- Community links in settings: report a bug, suggest an idea or follow updates through the StillCraft bot on Telegram.
